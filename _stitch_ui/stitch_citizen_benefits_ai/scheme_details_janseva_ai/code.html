Scheme Overview & Benefits

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana is a maternity benefit program run by the Government of India. It is a conditional cash transfer scheme for pregnant and lactating women of 19 years of age or above for the first live birth.

  • check_circle Nutritional support for mother and child.
  • check_circle Partial wage compensation during rest.
  • check_circle Incentivized institutional deliveries.
  • check_circle Encourages timely immunization.

Application Guide

1

Register at Anganwadi

Visit your nearest Anganwadi Centre (AWC) or approved Health Facility and register using Form 1A.

2

First Installment Registration

Submit Form 1A along with Aadhaar details and Bank Account details within 150 days of LMP.

3

Direct Benefit Transfer

Upon verification, the amount will be credited directly to your bank account via DBT.
